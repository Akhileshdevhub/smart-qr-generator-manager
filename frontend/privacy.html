Privacy notice

How scan analytics work, in plain terms.

What we collect when a dynamic QR is scanned

When someone scans a dynamic QR code, the server records one scan event containing:

  • A timestamp.
  • A device category (mobile, tablet, desktop, or bot).
  • The browser family and operating system family (e.g. "Chrome", "iOS").
  • An optional coarse country/region, when available.
  • A one-way hashed identifier derived from the IP address.

What we do not store

  • We do not store the raw IP address. We store only a salted SHA-256 hash truncated to 16 characters, which cannot be reversed back to an address.
  • We do not store precise location, names, or any personal profile.
  • Static QR codes are never tracked at all — they don't touch our server when scanned.

Why and how long

The purpose is to give a QR's owner a useful, aggregate picture of how their code is being used (how many scans, on what kind of device) without profiling individual people. Events are kept for the life of the QR project and are deleted when the project is deleted.

This is a student portfolio project; the analytics are intentionally minimal. Limitations (bot noise, approximate device detection) are documented in the project's docs/limitations.md.
